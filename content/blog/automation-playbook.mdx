Founder teams scale faster when repetitive tasks are automated before hiring aggressively.

## 30-day plan

1. Identify top 3 repetitive workflows.
2. Instrument lead lifecycle events.
3. Automate qualification + handoff.

Focus on throughput, not tool count.
